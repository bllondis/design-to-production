<Section label="Problem" title="A product model for regulated filing work">
  DocuStream was a greenfield product. We had legal rules, business requirements, client requests and established filing
  processes — but no product interaction model yet.
</Section>

<Section label="Approach" title="Translate requirements into interaction rules">
  I worked with the Design Lead, PM/BA, engineers and client-side filing specialists to translate those inputs into
  interaction rules: what information was required, what changed by jurisdiction or filing state, what needed
  explanation, and which actions should become available next.
</Section>

<Principle text="Reveal complexity when it becomes actionable." />

<Section label="Filing creation" title="One filing system, two levels of guidance">
  The product intentionally supports two parallel filing paths instead of forcing every user through the same workflow.
</Section>

<Section label="Guided Wizard · Supporting pattern" title="Guidance without leaving the task">
  Filing rules often need explanation at the exact moment information is entered. The contextual instructions panel
  keeps guidance beside the active filing step, so users can reference legal information without losing form state or
  orientation.
</Section>

<Section label="Lifecycle design" title="Status is a behaviour system, not a badge">
  A filing continues long after submission. Its state determines what information matters, what needs attention and
  which actions become available.
</Section>

  interaction logic as the everyday path.

<Section label="Outcome" title="All workflows shown in this case shipped to production.">
  DocuStream established a consistent product model for creating, managing and acting on UCC filings across different levels of user expertise, filing states and jurisdiction-specific requirements.

The same interaction patterns provided a foundation for additional forms and workflows as the product expanded.

</Section>

<Section label="Reflection" title="What I took forward" />

<Cards columns={2}>
  <Card title="Domain knowledge is design material">
    In specialised products, understanding the rules changes the quality of every interaction decision — from labels and
    validation to warnings, actions and workflow structure.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Model the system before the screens">
    If I approached the project again, I would formalise states, conditional rules, warnings and available actions even
    earlier as a shared artifact for design, product and engineering.
  </Card>
</Cards>
